# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

College students and early-career candidates preparing for internship and entry-level interviews.

## Product Purpose

InterviewAI gives students a low-pressure place to practice behavioral and technical interviews, receive focused feedback, and see where to improve before a real interview.

## Positioning

It combines a guided mock-interview flow with feedback organized around interview-ready habits such as STAR structure, clarity, and confidence.

## Operating Context

Students use the product alone while preparing for career fairs, internship applications, and upcoming interviews. The first experience should let them select a target role and begin a practice session quickly.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Mock behavioral and technical interview practice
- Role and company selection
- Timed answers and answer history
- Feedback and improvement tracking
- Initial build uses local sample questions and feedback; AI/API feedback is a later integration

## Evidence on Hand

No user-study data, testimonials, or performance claims are available. The app will use clearly representative sample interview data.

## Product Principles

- Make practice feel approachable, not intimidating.
- Start a useful mock interview with minimal setup.
- Turn feedback into a concrete next action.
- Keep the student in control of their progress and data.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use clear labels, keyboard-accessible controls, readable contrast, and avoid implying that a score determines a candidate's worth.
